### My projects:

#### Walker: Reborn (https://www.roblox.com/pt/games/15547863893/Walker-Reborn)

**Objective**: Survive in a endless day-night cycle, where at night Walker comes out from 2 possible wells and starts lurking for victims to prey and eat. It is possible to kill it, but not for forever.

**Core Mechanics:**

*   **Custom AI Engine:** Advanced pathfinding, states for stalking, pursuit, retreat, and stealth interaction, utilizing modular AI behavior.
*   **Gameplay & Survival Framework:** State machines for Sanity, Hallucination, Dynamic Weather/Moon phases, and a custom Disease system.
*   **Combat & Physics Systems:** Custom Melee/Firearm framework featuring context-based heavy attacks, Stamina management, and QTE (Quick Time Event) combat.
*   **Inventory & Economy:** Robust Backpack/Loot system, dynamic environmental item spawning, and a data-stored Shop System.
*   **Atmosphere & Performance:** Custom horror post-processing and procedural horror events optimized for low replication lag and high server-client efficiency.

The project focuses heavily on gameplay systems, AI behavior, atmosphere, and player interaction, with most systems being developed from scratch in Luau.
